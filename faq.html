Frequently asked questions

Short, honest answers.

Questions

Does Ledgerline file GST, VAT or income tax returns?

No. It shows a deadline calendar. You or your adviser file the returns.

Does it replace a CA or CPA?

No. It is bookkeeping software, not tax, legal or investment advice.

How many clients can one login handle?

The Accountant plan covers 50 client workspaces per pack. More packs raise the limit.

Can my staff each have a login?

Not yet. There is one login per subscription and staff share it.

Which countries are supported?

India, the US, the UK, Canada, Australia, the UAE and Singapore.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. 14 days, and no card is needed to start.

How do I pay?

Online plans are paid by card at checkout. The paid pilot and annual pack are paid by invoice. See pricing.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Download CSV files, or export all data as JSON.

Are my data backed up? What is the uptime?

No backup or uptime guarantee is offered at this stage. Please export your data regularly.

Do you hold any security certification?

No certification is claimed. Ask us the technical questions you need answered, and we reply in writing.

Where do I ask a question?

Use the contact form.

How a licence works today

  • Licences are built from the real limits: 50 client workspaces per accountant pack, or one business per account. There are no team logins, so staff share one login per pack.
  • You receive a written quotation and, once agreed, a subscription agreement and invoice.
  • We activate the licensed business accounts ourselves after payment. Each business gets its own login and its own books. For accountant networks, workspaces are provided in packs of 50 clients.
  • Support is by email. There is no uptime or response-time guarantee unless one is agreed in writing.
  • Businesses can export their own data as CSV or JSON.

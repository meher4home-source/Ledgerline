Questions

Does it replace QuickBooks or Xero?

Not for everyone. Ledgerline handles your records, invoices, reminders, reports and the client link. It does not connect to your bank automatically yet, so you upload a bank statement file instead. Try it on one client first.

How do the reminders for unpaid invoices work?

If you switch them on, an unpaid invoice gets a polite email to your customer after a week, then once a week, up to three times.

What does my client see?

A page just for them: cash balance, income, spending, profit and unpaid invoices. They can look but not change anything. The link stops working after 90 days, and you can turn it off any time.

Is the client confirmation a legal signature?

No. The client types their name and the time is saved. It is a simple confirmation.

Which countries are supported?

US, UK, Europe, Canada, Australia, UAE, Singapore and India, with each country's tax name, financial-year start and filing calendar. Everywhere else works too, with a simple general setup.

Can I pay in my own currency?

Yes, for the countries we list: US dollars, pounds, euros, Singapore dollars, UAE dirhams or Indian rupees, based on the country you choose when you sign up. Other countries pay in US dollars.

What happens after the trial?

You choose a plan to keep adding data. Your existing data stays visible.

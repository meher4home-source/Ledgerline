The product, as it is

What Ledgerline does not do

  • It does not file GST, TDS, VAT, income tax or any other return. The filing calendar only shows due dates.
  • It is not tax, legal or investment advice and does not replace a CA or CPA.
  • There are no bank feeds yet. Bank statements are imported as CSV files.
  • There are no team logins yet. Each subscription is one login.
  • The client confirmation records a name and a time. It is not a legal e-signature.

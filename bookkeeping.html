What it gives a firm

Separate client books

Every client is a workspace with its own entries, invoices, inventory, assets and statements.

50 workspaces per pack

Buy more packs to raise the limit. Pricing is on the pricing page.

Seven countries

Clients in India, the US, the UK, Canada, Australia, the UAE and Singapore, with country-specific deadline calendars.

Client reports and read-only links

Prepare a report for a client, or share a read-only link so the client can review and confirm.

Documents and export

Store scans and bills per client. Export data as CSV or JSON at any time.

Limits that matter to a firm

  • One login per subscription. Your staff share that login. There are no team logins, roles or audit logs yet. If this blocks you, say so before you buy.
  • Clients do not get their own logins on your plan. They can view a read-only link.
  • There are no bank feeds. Bank statements are imported as CSV.
  • It does not file GST, TDS, VAT, income tax or any other return. The filing calendar only shows due dates.
  • It is not tax, legal or investment advice and does not replace a CA or CPA.
  • No uptime, backup or security-certification commitments are offered at this stage. Export your data regularly.

Questions a firm should ask us

Where data is stored, how it is separated between clients, what happens if you leave, and how support works. Ask in the enquiry form. We answer in writing and say plainly what we cannot confirm.

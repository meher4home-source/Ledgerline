What the Accountant plan gives you

Separate client books

Each client is a workspace with its own entries, invoices, inventory and statements.

Monthly client report

Read-only sharing

Documents per client

Export

Limits you should know

  • It is not tax, legal or investment advice and does not replace a CA or CPA.
